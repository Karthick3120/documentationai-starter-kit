## Quick Start

To create your first documentation space:

1. Login to your Karthick K dashboard
2. Click **+ New Space** on the left sidebar
3. Enter a name for your documentation (e.g., "Team Wiki")
4. Click **Create**
5. Your new space now appears in the sidebar

You can start adding pages and organizing content within the space.

<Callout kind="info">
Each space helps you separate docs for different projects or teams.
</Callout>
