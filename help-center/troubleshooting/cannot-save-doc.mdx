---
title: Cannot Save Documentation
description: Fix issues when saving your Karthick K docs.
---

## Symptoms

- The save button is disabled
- Error: "Unable to save changes"

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check connectivity">
    ````bash
    ping dashboard.karthickk.com
    ````
    Ensure you are online.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refresh session">
    Log out and log in again to refresh your session.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear browser cache">
    Try clearing your browser cache and cookies.
  </Step>
</Steps>
