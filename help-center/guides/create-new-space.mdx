---
title: Creating a New Documentation Space
description: How to set up a project space in Karthick K.
---

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Dashboard">
    Log in at https://dashboard.karthickk.com and go to your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Space">
    Click **+ New Space** at the top left.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name Your Space">
    Enter a name and select a visibility (private or public).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Members (Optional)">
    Invite team members by email to collaborate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Writing">
    Your new space is ready—add docs or import existing markdown files.
  </Step>
</Steps>
