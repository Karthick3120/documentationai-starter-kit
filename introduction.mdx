## Welcome to Karthick K Docs

This starter kit gives you a clean space to organize product notes, team knowledge, process guides, and project references in one place. You can adapt the structure to fit your workflow, whether you write for a small team or a growing organization.

Use this hub to map your documentation into clear sections, keep important information easy to find, and help readers move from overview to detail without friction. The goal is simple: make your documentation space feel organized, useful, and easy to extend.
